    Morning briefing

    A short morning briefing: where you left off, today's calendar and weather, open threads. It leaves a card on Flash, sends a phone notification if those are on, and can say it on the Mac when you first sit down (never to an empty room, not after noon).

    Check-ins

    Now and then, while you are at the Mac, CODEC's local model looks at your next two hours, due follow-ups, important unread mail, failing services and your checklist. It stays quiet unless something needs you; then it leaves a card on Flash. Nothing leaves the Mac.
