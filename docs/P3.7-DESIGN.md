# P3.7 — 'CODEC is watching' indicator with pause

Item 25 of docs/UI-PHASE2-3-PLAN.md. Stacked on P3.6 (#433).

## Why

The observer (Phase 2 Step 5) reads the screen, the frontmost window, the clipboard type and recent files every
minute, and nothing on the pages says so. It can only be stopped with an environment variable and a restart. The
debug read `/api/observer/buffer` runs in the dashboard, not in the observer, so it always shows an empty buffer.
And `/api/triggers` is defined twice (the voice-trigger guide in routes/skills.py and the Step 6 trigger list in
routes/triggers.py); the guide wins, so the trigger list and its kill switch cannot be reached from a page. Even
then, a kill written by the dashboard would not reach the observer, which caches the kill and mute files forever.

## What

**Indicator.** An eye button in every page's header while the observer is watching (its disk mirror was written in
the last 11 minutes) or paused. Its menu:

- **Pause for 15 minutes / 1 hour / until tomorrow (6:00)**: writes `~/.codec/observer_paused_until` (one ISO time,
  0600). The daemon reads it on every loop: while it is in the future the observer does not poll, forgets its RAM
  buffer and deletes the disk mirror (once), and the chat's observation summary is not injected. The shift report
  still fires on time. **Resume watching** deletes the flag. Both are audited (`observer_paused`, `observer_resumed`).
- **What CODEC sees now**: reads `~/.codec/observer_buffer.json` (the mirror the observer writes) and shows metadata
  only: the frontmost app, how long the window title and the screen text are, the clipboard's type and length, how
  many files changed, how many snapshots are kept and when. No titles, screen text, clipboard text or paths. The read
  is audited like the debug endpoint (`observer_buffer_inspected`).
- **Automatic triggers**: the Step 6 list (each trigger's summary, skill, type) with **Mute** (soft, through
  `~/.codec/triggers.json`, still logged as `trigger_muted`) and **Turn off** (`~/.codec/triggers_killed.json`).
- **Observer settings**: Settings.

**Route clash.** The voice-trigger guide moves to `/api/voice_triggers` (GET and POST; Home's Skills page and
`scripts/feature_audit.py` follow), so `/api/triggers` is the Step 6 list again. A new `POST
/api/triggers/{key}/mute` sets or clears a skill's mute (written whole, as the file's contract says; the default mute
stays), and the list reports `muted`.

**Kill and mute reach the observer.** `codec_triggers` reloads the kill and mute files when they change on disk (by
modification time) instead of caching them until a restart.

## API

- `GET /api/observer/state` → `{watching, paused_until, updated, entries}`.
- `POST /api/observer/pause` `{minutes: 15|60}` or `{until: "tomorrow"}` → `{paused_until}`; `POST /api/observer/resume`.
- `GET /api/observer/now` → `{updated, entries, latest: {app, title_length, screen_text_length, clipboard: {type,
  length}, recent_files}}`.
- `GET/POST /api/voice_triggers` (was `/api/triggers` in routes/skills.py). `POST /api/triggers/{key}/mute` `{muted}`.

No change to the observer's polling, its RAM buffer contract, the audit envelope or the security lists. The
`OBSERVER_ENABLED` kill switch is unchanged.
