# P3.4 — 'Today' home instead of the Flash log

Item 23 of docs/UI-PHASE2-3-PLAN.md. Stacked on P3.2 (#431). Decision of 29 Sep (DECISIONS.md): Home becomes
"Today" (briefing card, Needs you, next events, open threads, agents); the Flash box stays at the bottom and History
stays reachable.

## Why

Home opens on the Flash log. What the owner wants first — what needs them, what is next today, what they are in
the middle of, what the agents did — is spread over the Inbox, the briefing card, the calendar skill and Tasks.

## What

Home's first tab is renamed **Today**. Above the existing cards (briefing, schedules, check-ins) and the Flash
conversation, a Today section shows, in order:

- **Greeting**: "Good morning / afternoon / evening" and the date (no name).
- **Needs you**: the Inbox's Needs-you items with the same cards and actions (approvals, questions, blocked agents),
  from the shell's one Inbox poll (`CodecShell.inbox.mount`). Hidden when empty.
- **Next up**: the next 3 events of today from the existing `google_calendar` skill, run through the chat's
  explicit-skill path (allowlist, consent, hooks, audit) by a new read route and cached for 5 minutes. When the
  calendar is not connected it says so, with a link to Connections.
- **Open threads** (working on / waiting on / follow up) from a new read route over the `thread:*` facts
  (`codec_daybreak.get_open_threads`), each with Close (the existing `/api/today/threads/done`), Snooze for a day
  (new; `~/.codec/thread_snooze.json`, 0600, never touches memory.db) and Draft reply for waiting-on and follow-up.
- **Agents**: running, paused or blocked agents, and the ones that finished in the last 24 hours, with their status;
  a blocked one points at the Inbox.
- **Yesterday**: the first lines of yesterday's shift report, with Read (opens it in Chat via `#report=`).
- **Quick tiles**: Now playing, Pause, Play, Lights relax, Lights off, Volume down, Volume up, Timer (asks how many
  minutes), Screenshot (the page's existing viewer). Each runs one fixed skill and task on the server
  (`POST /api/today/tile`): the client sends only a tile name, never a skill or text.

The briefing card gains **Replay**: it reads the card aloud on this device (P2.8's read-aloud). The Flash composer
stays at the bottom and History stays the next tab: no working feature is lost.

## API

- `GET /api/today/home` → `{threads, agents, yesterday}`.
- `GET /api/today/next` → `{events: [{time, title}], connected, note}` (cached 5 minutes).
- `POST /api/today/threads/snooze` `{key, hours}` (1-168).
- `POST /api/today/tile` `{tile, minutes?}`: `now_playing`, `music_pause`, `music_resume`, `lights_relax`,
  `lights_off`, `volume_down`, `volume_up`, `timer` (1-240 minutes).
- `CodecShell.inbox.mount(element, group)`: draws that Inbox group's cards into a page element, with the drawer's
  actions.

No change to memory.db, notifications.json, the security lists or any skill.

## Test plan

`tests/test_today_home.py`: the home summary (threads without snoozed ones, agents by status and age, yesterday's
report lines), snooze validation and expiry, the calendar parse (future events only, three at most, all-day first,
not connected), the cache, tiles (fixed skill and task per tile, unknown tile and bad minutes refused, nothing else
reaches the skill), and the page wiring (tab renamed, sections, mount, Replay, composer and History kept). Under
jsdom: `CodecShell.inbox.mount` draws a group's cards and posts their actions.

Mac test before merge: Today at 1470x956 and 375x812, light theme; real calendar events; a thread snoozed and
closed; tiles with Spotify/Music, Hue and the timer; Flash still sends; console clean.

## Rollback

Revert the PR: Home opens on Flash again; the new routes and the snooze file are unused.
