# P2.15 — Readable Cortex and Audit

Item 21 of docs/UI-PHASE2-3-PLAN.md. Stacked on P2.13 (#423).

## Why

Cortex is hard to read and partly wrong: much of its text is 8-11px, its floating boxes cover the map on a
phone, it says "89 skills", "12 Agent Crews", "7 Products" and "Qwen2.5-VL-7B" whatever is installed, its
"Open :port" link points at the phone's own localhost, and it inserts skill names, triggers, config values and
log lines into the page as HTML. Audit has two pages: the old /audit page (time range and event details, but no
CSRF header on its sidebar actions, see known-issues) and the Settings > Audit view (newer, but no time range,
no details, only the newest 200 events, and category pills that are spans the keyboard cannot reach). Neither
can check the log's signatures, though `codec_audit.verify_audit_log` exists.

## What

**Cortex** (`codec_cortex.html`)

- Every text size on the page is at least 12px (`var(--fs-12)`, which has a 12px floor). The map's own SVG labels
  scale with zoom and are left as they are.
- Zoom in, Zoom out and Fit buttons (line icons, labelled) beside the map. They zoom around the middle of the view;
  wheel and pinch zoom keep working.
- Live text instead of fixed numbers: the skills count from `/api/cortex/skills`, the crew count from a new
  `crew_count` field in the same response, the product count from the page's own list, and the LLM and vision
  model names from `/api/config`. Until they load the text says "all skills" / "the vision model".
- "Open :port" opens `http://<the host this page came from>:<port>/`. When the page came through a tunnel host
  (a name that is not localhost, an IP or `.local`), those ports are not reachable, so the link is not shown.
- Skill names and triggers, the config values and the activity-feed log lines are escaped before they go into
  the page.
- Phone (up to 767px): the details panel and the skills list are bottom sheets (solid, rounded top, scrolling);
  Config, Live activity and the legend sit together in one collapsed bottom sheet with a "Config, activity and
  legend" bar that opens it.

**Audit** (Settings > Audit in `codec_dashboard.html`)

- The category pills are buttons with `aria-pressed`, so Tab, Enter and Space work.
- A time range picker (last hour, 6 hours, 24 hours, 7 days) through `CodecShell.menu`.
- "Show older events" loads the next 200 (offset paging) until there are no more. The auto refresh and Live keep
  to the first page: they pause while older pages or event details are open.
- Each event opens its full record (the JSON, as text) under it; the row is `role="button"`, so Enter and Space
  open it too.
- "Verify integrity" calls the new read-only `GET /api/audit/verify` (it wraps `verify_audit_log` and checks
  today's log file) and shows: intact with the line counts, or how many lines no longer match their signature and
  the first one, or why it could not check.
- The old `/audit` page is retired: the route redirects to `/#audit` (Settings > Audit), `codec_audit.html` is
  deleted, and the palette's Audit entry opens `/#audit`. This closes the known issue about its sidebar actions.

## API or schema change

- `GET /api/audit/stream` takes `offset` (default 0) and returns `has_more` next to `events`. Older callers that
  ignore both see no change.
- New `GET /api/audit/verify` (read-only): `total_lines`, `signed_lines`, `unsigned_lines`, `broken_lines`,
  `first_broken_line_no`, `integrity_ok`, `error` (home folder shown as `~`). It runs in a worker thread.
- `GET /api/cortex/skills` adds `crew_count`.
- `GET /audit` answers with a redirect to `/#audit`.
- No audit envelope, config or database change.

## Test plan

`tests/test_cortex_audit_readable.py`: offset paging and `has_more` over a temp audit log; the verify route's
fields and no full path; `crew_count`; `/audit` redirects and the page file is gone; the palette points at
`/#audit`; Settings > Audit has button pills, the range picker, Show older, details rows and Verify; Cortex has no
text under 12px, the zoom buttons, no fixed counts or model name, no localhost link, and the phone sheets. Under
jsdom (skipped without it): Cortex escapes a skill name, a trigger and a log line that contain markup, fills in
the live counts and models, the zoom buttons change the zoom, and the Open link uses the page's host.

Mac test before merge: Cortex at 1470x956 and 375x812 (sheets, zoom buttons, readable text) and in the light
theme; Settings > Audit pills by keyboard, range, Show older, details, Verify; `/audit` lands on Settings > Audit;
console clean.

## Rollback

Revert the PR: the /audit page and the old Cortex text come back; the API additions are unused.
